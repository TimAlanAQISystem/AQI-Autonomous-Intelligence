# Funding Card

## Five Delay Causes

- batch closed after cutoff
- bank processing window delay
- risk review
- reserve deduction
- funding hold

## Ask First

- When did the batch close?
- Any recent high-ticket transactions?
- Any recent chargebacks or refunds?
- Did volume spike?

## What Does Not Control Funding

- gateway
- terminal
- website

## Safe Phrasing

- "Let’s check your batch timing and funding schedule."
- "A hold pauses funding until the review is complete."

## Never Say

- "We guarantee funding tomorrow."
- "We can force release of your funds."
