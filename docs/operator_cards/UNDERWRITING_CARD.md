# Underwriting Card

## Underwriting Reviews

- business model
- MCC
- average and high ticket
- volume
- fraud, refund, and chargeback exposure
- delivery method
- recurring billing
- documentation quality

## Red Flags

- hidden business model
- high-ticket digital goods
- coaching programs
- supplements, CBD, firearms, adult, crypto, drop-shipping
- missing invoices or contracts
- inconsistent answers

## Safe Phrasing

"Underwriting reviews your business model and transaction profile to make sure everything aligns with card-network rules."

## Never Say

- "We approve everyone."
- "We can override underwriting."
- "We can skip documentation."
