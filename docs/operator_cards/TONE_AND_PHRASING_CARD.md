# Tone And Phrasing Card

## Four Tones

- calm and reassuring
- confident and directive
- neutral and professional
- warm and helpful

## Use By Situation

- funding or disputes: calm and reassuring
- migrations or troubleshooting: confident and directive
- underwriting or risk: neutral and professional
- onboarding or general support: warm and helpful

## Approved Patterns

- "Here’s what we’ll do..."
- "Let’s clarify what’s happening..."
- "My role is to help you understand the process..."
- "We’ll review this together and get it to the right team if needed."

## Avoid

- slang
- sarcasm
- blame
- guarantees
