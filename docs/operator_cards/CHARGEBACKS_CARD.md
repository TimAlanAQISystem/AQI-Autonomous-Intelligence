# Chargebacks Card

## Three Root Causes

- fraud
- service or product issue
- processing error

## Fast Reason-Code Families

- Visa: `10.x`, `12.x`, `13.x`
- Mastercard: `48xx`, `46xx`, `49xx`
- Amex: `F`, `C`, `P`

## Evidence That Works

- AVS and CVV
- 3-D Secure
- tracking and delivery proof
- signed service agreement
- refund and communication logs
- authorization and batch logs

## Evidence That Fails

- "The customer is lying."
- "We know this customer."
- "They signed up willingly."

## Operator Anchor

"The bank only accepts evidence that matches the reason code."

## Escalate When

- merchant wants guaranteed win
- repeated fraud exposure
- arbitration discussion is needed
