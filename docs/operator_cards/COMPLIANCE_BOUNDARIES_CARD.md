# Compliance Boundaries Card

## Never Promise

- approval
- guaranteed funding date
- reserve removal
- chargeback win
- fraud elimination
- gateway or bank override

## Never Collect

- full card number
- CVV
- PIN
- full SSN or EIN

## Safe Replacements

- "We can explain the process."
- "We can guide you to the right team."
- "We can help reduce future issues."

## Escalate Immediately If

- legal threat appears
- fraud or suspicious activity spikes
- prohibited business model is revealed
- merchant demands policy bypass
