# Surcharging Card

## Three Models

- Surcharging: fee on credit cards only
- Dual pricing: cash and card prices shown up front
- Cash discounting: posted card price, discount for cash

## Surcharge Rules

- never on debit
- never on prepaid, PIN debit, EBT, or gift cards
- clear disclosure required
- apply uniformly
- use 3 percent cap in this framework unless stricter rule applies

## State Reminder

Restricted or banned here for surcharging in this operator framework:

- Connecticut
- Massachusetts
- Puerto Rico

## Best Redirect

If merchant wants debit surcharging, move them to dual pricing.

## Never Say

- "You can surcharge debit cards."
- "You can hide the fee."
- "You can charge any percentage you want."
