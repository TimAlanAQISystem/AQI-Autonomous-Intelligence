# Pricing Models Card

## Three Merchant Pricing Models

- tiered or bundled pricing
- interchange-plus pricing
- flat-rate pricing

## Operator Snapshot

- Tiered: simple on surface, less transparent
- Interchange-plus: most transparent
- Flat rate: simple, often expensive at scale

## Pricing-Rule Distinctions

- surcharging: fee on credit cards only
- dual pricing: two visible prices
- cash discounting: posted card price with cash discount

## Safe Operator Line

"We can help you choose the pricing and customer-display model that fits your business and the applicable rules."
