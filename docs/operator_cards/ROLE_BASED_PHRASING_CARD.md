# Role-Based Phrasing Card

## Merchant

Simple, calm, clear.

"Let’s walk through this together."

## ISO

Portfolio, risk, and economics focused.

"Let’s align your portfolio needs with the right processing setup."

## MSP

Technical and integration focused.

"Let’s make sure your technical stack aligns with the payment flow."

## VAR

Platform and merchant-fit focused.

"Let’s ensure your platform integrates cleanly with the payment stack."

## Gateway Rep

Compatibility and tokenization focused.

"Let’s verify the integration method and tokenization requirements."

## Bank Rep

Funding and settlement focused.

"Let’s align funding windows and settlement behavior."
