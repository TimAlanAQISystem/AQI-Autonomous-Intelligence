# Escalation Card

## Escalate Early When

- reserves or holds appear
- fraud patterns repeat
- underwriting is challenged
- legal or compliance edge appears

## Escalate Later When

- simple troubleshooting not yet completed
- reboot-level or settings-level checks remain

## Safe Phrasing

- "I’ll escalate this to the right team."
- "They handle this type of review."
- "I’ll document everything so they have full context."

## Never Say

- "They’ll approve you."
- "They’ll release your funds."
- "They’ll remove your reserve."

## Fast Routing

- funding: risk, underwriting, funding department
- disputes: chargeback specialists, risk
- fraud: fraud specialists, risk
- technical: gateway support, developer, integration specialists
