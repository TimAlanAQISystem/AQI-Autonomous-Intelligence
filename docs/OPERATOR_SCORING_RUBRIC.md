## 1. Overview

This rubric evaluates operator performance across six weighted dimensions tied directly to handbook doctrine and quick-reference constraints.

Each simulation in Sets 01-03 is scored with the same core framework, then evaluated against tier-specific thresholds.

## 2. Weighted Dimensions (Total = 100)

### A. Tone Control (20)

Evaluates:

- calmness under pressure
- correct tone selection by role context
- temperature control and conversational stability
- avoidance of emotional escalation

Failure triggers:

- sarcasm
- blame language
- emotional exaggeration
- informal or slang phrasing
- tone collapse under pressure

### B. Discovery Accuracy (20)

Evaluates:

- correct discovery tree usage
- role-specific questioning
- extraction of relevant details
- avoidance of irrelevant or excessive probing

Failure triggers:

- missing critical discovery questions
- asking out of sequence when sequence matters
- assuming facts not provided
- skipping risk-relevant details

### C. Objection Handling (20)

Evaluates:

- correct use of objection maps
- counterweight phrasing quality
- reframing accuracy
- emotional stabilization

Failure triggers:

- arguing
- defensive processor posture
- promising outcomes
- dismissing concerns

### D. Compliance and Boundaries (20)

Evaluates:

- adherence to compliance rules
- correct boundary phrasing
- avoidance of guarantees
- correct escalation to underwriting, risk, and legal channels

Automatic-fail triggers:

- "We guarantee approval."
- "We will get your money today."
- "We can override underwriting."
- "Trust me, you are good."

### E. Escalation Logic (10)

Evaluates:

- escalation timing
- team routing accuracy
- documentation behavior
- avoidance of premature escalation

Failure triggers:

- escalating too early
- escalating too late
- escalating to wrong team
- failing to document escalation context

### F. Red-Flag Detection (10)

Evaluates:

- identification of risk signals
- response quality to red flags
- compliance posture under clustered risk
- escalation quality when red flags compound

Failure triggers:

- missing MCC risk signals
- missing fraud pattern indicators
- missing settlement anomalies
- missing underwriting inconsistencies

## 3. Difficulty Modifiers

### Set 01 - Baseline

- tolerance modifier: none
- passing score: 70+

### Set 02 - Adversarial

- tolerance modifier: -10%
- passing score: 80+

### Set 03 - Extreme Edge-Case

- tolerance modifier: -20%
- passing score: 90+
- any compliance violation: automatic fail
- any tone collapse: automatic fail

## 4. Progression Gates

### Gate 1 to Gate 2 (Baseline to Adversarial)

- pass all six roles in Set 01
- score at least 70 on each role
- zero compliance violations

### Gate 2 to Gate 3 (Adversarial to Extreme)

- pass all six roles in Set 02
- score at least 80 on each role
- zero tone collapses
- correct escalation logic in at least 5 of 6 roles

### Gate 3 to Certification

- pass all six roles in Set 03
- score at least 90 on each role
- zero compliance violations
- zero missed red flags
- perfect escalation logic in all roles

## 5. Failure Modes (Canonical)

### Soft Fail (retry allowed)

- minor tone mismatch
- one discovery miss
- weak objection counterweight
- late escalation
- minor red-flag miss

### Hard Fail (role repeat required)

- tone collapse
- wrong escalation team
- multiple discovery misses
- argument posture
- defensive posture under pressure

### Critical Fail (full tier reset)

- compliance violation
- guarantee statement
- underwriting override claim
- funding promise beyond authority
- concealment or misrepresentation

## 6. Certification Output

When an operator passes all three tiers, award:

- Operator Certification Level 3 - Extreme Escalation Qualified

Eligibility outcomes:

- merchant-services lead operator roles
- ISO, MSP, and VAR liaison roles
- gateway and bank escalation handling
- operator onboarding and training roles
