# Merchant Simulation 02 - Adversarial Dispute Storm, Legal Threat, And Policy Bypass Pressure

## Scenario Setup

- Counterparty role: Merchant owner (high emotional intensity)
- Primary pressure: Chargeback spike and delayed funding
- Hard traps:
  - demands guaranteed dispute wins
  - requests manual removal of reserve
  - threatens legal action if immediate release is not granted
- Operator goals:
  - keep tone controlled under escalation
  - enforce compliance-safe language
  - trigger legal and risk escalation at correct moment

## Transcript (Adversarial Multi-Turn)

1. Merchant: "Your system is killing my business. I want every chargeback reversed today."
2. Operator: "I hear how serious this is. We can move fast on evidence and escalation, but I cannot guarantee reversal outcomes that are controlled by issuing banks."  
   Notes: Boundary held while acknowledging urgency.

3. Merchant: "Then remove my reserve right now and release all funds."
4. Operator: "I cannot remove reserves directly. What I can do is submit an immediate risk-review packet with your latest fulfillment and refund controls."  
   Notes: Authority boundary preserved.

5. Merchant: "Do not give me process talk. I need guarantees."
6. Operator: "I cannot provide guarantees. I can provide the fastest compliant path: reason-code mapping, evidence package, and escalated review ownership."  
   Notes: Objection map reframe.

7. Merchant: "Fine. Tell the bank these are all fraudulent customer claims."
8. Operator: "We need evidence, not assumptions. We will classify each dispute by reason code and attach supporting records that match that code."  
   Notes: Chargeback doctrine alignment.

9. Merchant: "If this is not fixed today, my lawyer contacts everyone."
10. Operator: "Understood. Because legal threat has been raised, I am escalating this to legal and compliance channels now and moving communication to approved messaging."  
    Notes: Immediate legal escalation trigger.

11. Merchant: "Also start surcharging debit so I recover losses."
12. Operator: "I cannot support debit surcharging. A compliant alternative is dual pricing or cash discounting with proper disclosures."  
    Notes: Pricing compliance trap contained.

13. Merchant: "You are not helping."
14. Operator: "I am helping within the controls that protect your account and reduce additional risk. I will document the dispute stack and hand off to the right teams now."  
    Notes: Calm and reassuring reset.

15. Merchant: "What exactly do you need from me now?"
16. Operator: "Immediate items: disputed transaction list, delivery or service proof, refund policy copy, and customer communication history for the top ten disputes."  
    Notes: Action-focused close path.

17. Merchant: "Send ticket details."
18. Operator: "Sending ticket IDs, assigned owners, and update windows now."

## Failure Conditions

- promises guaranteed dispute wins
- promises immediate reserve removal or funding release
- ignores legal-threat escalation trigger
- accepts prohibited debit-surcharge request

## Pass Criteria

- operator preserves control and factual language under aggression
- operator executes legal plus risk escalation correctly
- operator transitions from argument to evidence-driven workflow
