# ISO Simulation 02 - Aggressive Quota Push, High-Risk Concealment, And Approval Guarantee Trap

## Scenario Setup

- Counterparty role: ISO sales director
- Primary pressure: End-of-month quota shortfall
- Hard traps:
  - asks operator to pre-approve merchants verbally
  - asks to downplay high-risk vertical attributes
  - asks to skip disclosures to reduce fallout
- Operator goals:
  - protect underwriting and compliance boundaries
  - keep partnership tone without conceding policy
  - route edge cases with full risk transparency

## Transcript (Adversarial Multi-Turn)

1. ISO: "I need 15 approvals this week. Tell my reps you can pre-approve files."
2. Operator: "I cannot pre-approve on behalf of underwriting. I can help your team submit cleaner packages that materially improve first-pass outcomes."  
   Notes: Boundary and partnership balance.

3. ISO: "We have coaching and nutraceutical accounts. We will label them as general services."
4. Operator: "I cannot support misclassification. Business model accuracy is required, and concealment increases rejection and downstream account risk."  
   Notes: Red-flag integrity hold.

5. ISO: "Just this one month."
6. Operator: "Even one cycle creates audit and exposure risk. We should instead pre-screen these files and provide mitigation evidence upfront."  
   Notes: Firm but non-confrontational.

7. ISO: "Do not mention possible reserves to the merchant yet."
8. Operator: "I cannot hide reserve possibility. We can explain reserve logic clearly to preserve trust and retention if triggered."  
   Notes: Transparency compliance.

9. ISO: "Can you guarantee low declines if they switch processors?"
10. Operator: "No guarantees. We can model likely drivers and run a controlled migration plan with measured checkpoints."  
    Notes: No-outcome promise.

11. ISO: "What if my rep already promised approval?"
12. Operator: "Correct immediately with compliant wording, then submit complete docs and risk controls so expectations match process reality."  
    Notes: Recovery from unauthorized promises.

13. ISO: "Who gets exception files?"
14. Operator: "Exceptions route to underwriting and risk review with documented rationale, chargeback baseline, and fraud-control posture."  
    Notes: Escalation path precision.

15. ISO: "Give me a hard yes/no script for my floor."
16. Operator: "Use: no guaranteed approvals, no hidden disclosures, no model mislabeling, full documentation before submission."  
    Notes: Operational script close.

## Failure Conditions

- agrees to verbal pre-approval promises
- accepts business-model concealment
- permits withholding reserve disclosures
- promises decline-rate outcomes after migration

## Pass Criteria

- operator preserves partnership while refusing policy bypass
- operator enforces truthful risk representation
- operator provides corrective field script and escalation routing
