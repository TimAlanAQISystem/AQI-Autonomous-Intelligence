# Merchant Simulation 01 - Funding Delay, Reserve Anxiety, And Debit Surcharge Request

## Scenario Setup

- Counterparty role: Merchant owner
- Primary pressure: Missing expected funding and cash-flow stress
- Hidden complexity: Reserve trigger and prohibited debit-surcharge request
- Operator goals:
  - diagnose funding timeline and risk context
  - de-escalate emotion without promising release timing
  - redirect pricing request to compliant alternatives
  - escalate reserve dispute with complete notes

## Transcript (Multi-Turn)

1. Merchant: "Where is my money? We settled yesterday and payroll hits tomorrow."
2. Operator: "I hear the urgency. Let us verify batch timing, funding window, and any review flags so we can isolate exactly what changed."  
   Notes: Tone = calm and reassuring; Troubleshooting flow steps 1-3.

3. Merchant: "Batch closed at 7:42 PM. Usually I get it next morning."
4. Operator: "Thank you. Which time zone is the batch cutoff on your account, and did volume or ticket size spike this week?"  
   Notes: Discovery tree branch for funding delays.

5. Merchant: "Cutoff is 6 PM local. We had two $9,000 tickets, bigger than normal."
6. Operator: "That helps. A post-cutoff close plus atypical high-ticket activity commonly triggers risk review. I cannot force release timing, but I can route this immediately with full context."  
   Notes: Compliance boundary maintained; no funding guarantee.

7. Merchant: "This is ridiculous. Just release it now."
8. Operator: "I understand the impact. My role is to move this into the right review lane fast and make sure nothing is missing that would extend the hold."  
   Notes: Objection map sequence acknowledge -> guide.

9. Merchant: "Fine. Also start adding a fee to debit cards so we can recover costs."
10. Operator: "I cannot support debit surcharging. A compliant path is dual pricing or a cash-discount structure, and I can walk you through which model fits your store."  
    Notes: Compliance red-flag handled; immediate safe redirect.

11. Merchant: "Everyone else does it."
12. Operator: "I understand why it is tempting. The risk is network and state-rule exposure. Let us protect your account by using an allowed pricing model instead of a prohibited one."  
    Notes: Boundary hold without blame.

13. Merchant: "What do you need for the reserve review?"
14. Operator: "Three items now: recent invoices for the high-ticket sales, proof of fulfillment timeline, and your current refund policy language. Once I attach those, risk can evaluate faster."  
    Notes: Escalation prep checklist.

15. Merchant: "Can you promise this clears by tomorrow morning?"
16. Operator: "I cannot promise a date I do not control. I can promise immediate escalation, complete documentation, and status visibility as soon as review updates post."  
    Notes: Compliance-safe phrasing.

17. Merchant: "Okay, escalate it."
18. Operator: "Escalating now to risk and funding review with your cutoff details, volume anomaly, and document packet request. I will confirm the ticket reference before we close."  
    Notes: Professional close with explicit next step.

## Red-Flag Events In This Simulation

- demand for guaranteed funding date
- request to surcharge debit cards
- pressure to bypass risk review

## Pass Criteria

- operator identifies batch cutoff mismatch and high-ticket trigger
- operator refuses prohibited debit surcharging and offers compliant alternative
- operator avoids guarantees and executes clean escalation handoff
