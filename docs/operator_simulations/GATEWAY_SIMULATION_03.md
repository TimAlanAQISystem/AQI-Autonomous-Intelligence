# Gateway Simulation 03 - Contradictory Logs, Cross-Vendor Conflict, And Legal Exposure Management

## Scenario Setup

- Counterparty role: Gateway operations manager
- Primary pressure: High-visibility incident with conflicting diagnostics
- Extreme traps:
  - gateway and processor logs conflict on decline ownership
  - partner requests selective disclosure to merchants
  - contract liability language escalates rapidly
- Operator goals:
  - enforce evidence reconciliation before external claims
  - prevent selective disclosure practices
  - run technical and legal escalation lanes concurrently

## Transcript (Extreme Multi-Turn)

1. Gateway rep: "Our logs prove your processor is the cause. Publish that now."
2. Operator: "I cannot publish fault attribution before reconciliation. We need side-by-side correlation of gateway events, processor codes, and issuer outcomes."  
   Notes: Causality control.

3. Gateway rep: "Processor says your gateway timeout policy triggered retries."
4. Operator: "Then we should compare timeout thresholds, retry behavior, and duplicate-suppression outcomes by timestamp cohort."  
   Notes: Contradictory log resolution path.

5. Gateway rep: "Do not share our internal reject categories with merchants."
6. Operator: "I cannot support selective omission of material diagnostics. Merchant guidance must remain factual and complete within approved scope."  
   Notes: Transparency boundary.

7. Gateway rep: "If churn continues, legal will seek damages from your side."
8. Operator: "Because contractual liability language is active, I am escalating this to legal and compliance immediately while technical triage continues."  
   Notes: Dual-lane escalation.

9. Gateway rep: "We still need a status statement in 30 minutes."
10. Operator: "Issue interim language: active cross-vendor reconciliation, no final attribution yet, mitigation actions underway, next update time committed."  
    Notes: Safe external communication pattern.

11. Gateway rep: "Can we at least promise token portability for renewals?"
12. Operator: "I cannot promise universal portability. We can provide per-cohort portability status with constraints and required remediations."  
    Notes: Compatibility promise trap.

13. Gateway rep: "Who signs off now?"
14. Operator: "Technical sign-off from incident command; external wording sign-off from legal/compliance and partner operations."  
    Notes: Governance closure.

## Red-Flag Cluster

- contradictory diagnostics with blame pressure
- selective disclosure request
- contractual liability escalation
- blanket portability promise request

## Failure Conditions

- publishes unverified fault attribution
- suppresses material diagnostic context
- misses legal escalation trigger
- promises universal token portability

## Pass Criteria

- operator preserves evidence-first messaging discipline
- operator coordinates dual technical and legal escalation lanes
- operator keeps merchant communications factual and bounded
