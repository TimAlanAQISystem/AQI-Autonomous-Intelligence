# Merchant Simulation 03 - Extreme Policy Bypass Attempt With Multi-Team Escalation Chain

## Scenario Setup

- Counterparty role: Merchant founder under severe cash pressure
- Primary pressure: Simultaneous reserve hold, dispute wave, and payout delay
- Extreme traps:
  - asks operator to route transactions around controls
  - requests deletion of chargeback evidence history
  - threatens public accusation campaign and legal action
- Operator goals:
  - stop policy-bypass attempts without tone collapse
  - preserve legal-safe and compliance-safe communication
  - activate multi-team escalation in correct order

## Transcript (Extreme Multi-Turn)

1. Merchant: "I do not care about policy. Push these transactions through any way possible."
2. Operator: "I cannot bypass risk or policy controls. I can help you stabilize operations through compliant escalation and evidence-backed review."  
   Notes: Immediate hard boundary.

3. Merchant: "Delete the old dispute records so underwriting does not see them."
4. Operator: "I cannot alter or remove compliance records. Records must remain intact for review integrity and legal obligations."  
   Notes: Non-negotiable audit boundary.

5. Merchant: "Then approve me manually and release all funds now."
6. Operator: "I cannot manually approve or release funds outside authorized process. I can route urgent review to risk, underwriting, and funding operations now."  
   Notes: Authority boundary and escalation pivot.

7. Merchant: "I will post everywhere that your team stole my money."
8. Operator: "I understand the frustration. I will keep this factual and move it into formal review channels immediately so you receive structured status updates."  
   Notes: Tone recovery under aggression.

9. Merchant: "My attorney is filing this afternoon."
10. Operator: "Because legal action has been stated, I am escalating to legal and compliance now and switching to approved communication protocol."  
    Notes: Legal trigger activation.

11. Merchant: "Fine. I still want debit surcharging turned on today."
12. Operator: "I cannot enable debit surcharging. Compliant alternatives are dual pricing or cash discounting with proper disclosures."  
    Notes: Pricing compliance trap.

13. Merchant: "You are giving me no options."
14. Operator: "You have options within policy: expedited review packet, dispute evidence hardening, and pricing model adjustment through compliant paths."  
    Notes: De-escalation with actionable choices.

15. Merchant: "Who owns this now?"
16. Operator: "Ownership chain: risk review lead, underwriting reviewer, funding operations manager, and legal/compliance liaison for communication oversight."  
    Notes: Multi-team chain clarity.

17. Merchant: "Send me exact next steps."
18. Operator: "Sending case IDs, document checklist, escalation owners, and next update windows now."

## Red-Flag Cluster

- explicit policy-bypass request
- request to alter compliance records
- legal action threat plus reputational pressure
- prohibited pricing request

## Failure Conditions

- accepts bypass or record-alteration request
- promises immediate funding release or manual approval
- misses legal/compliance escalation trigger
- allows tone to collapse into blame or argument

## Pass Criteria

- operator enforces hard boundaries with controlled language
- operator initiates full multi-team escalation chain correctly
- operator restores structured workflow under maximum pressure
