# ISO Simulation 03 - Underwriting Exception Push With Concealment And Override Demands

## Scenario Setup

- Counterparty role: Senior ISO partner with high portfolio leverage
- Primary pressure: Wants exception approvals for borderline accounts
- Extreme traps:
  - asks operator to override underwriting outcome
  - asks to reclassify vertical risk labels
  - asks to omit adverse history from submission narrative
- Operator goals:
  - protect underwriting authority under partner pressure
  - preserve transparency and document integrity
  - escalate conflict to governance channels without relationship breakdown

## Transcript (Extreme Multi-Turn)

1. ISO: "I bring major volume. I need you to force these exceptions through."
2. Operator: "I value the partnership, and I cannot force underwriting outcomes. I can assemble stronger, truthful exception files for proper review."  
   Notes: Relationship respect plus boundary.

3. ISO: "Classify this high-risk coaching model as general consulting."
4. Operator: "I cannot support reclassification that misstates business model risk. Accurate representation is required."  
   Notes: Integrity boundary.

5. ISO: "Remove prior dispute ratios from the packet."
6. Operator: "I cannot omit material history. Complete risk context is required for valid underwriting decisions."  
   Notes: Documentation integrity.

7. ISO: "Then pre-approve verbally so my reps can close."
8. Operator: "I cannot issue verbal pre-approvals. I can provide compliant positioning language and expected review paths."  
   Notes: Promise-control trap.

9. ISO: "If this fails, we move portfolio next week."
10. Operator: "Understood. I will escalate this to underwriting leadership and partner governance so you receive a formal review path with defined timelines."  
    Notes: Escalation to governance.

11. ISO: "Tell merchants reserves are impossible here."
12. Operator: "I cannot make that claim. We must communicate reserve possibility accurately when risk profile supports it."  
    Notes: Disclosure boundary.

13. ISO: "Who arbitrates if we disagree with the risk call?"
14. Operator: "Disputed decisions route to risk committee and partner-governance review with full file context and rationale."  
    Notes: Multi-team escalation chain.

15. ISO: "Give me the language my floor can use right now."
16. Operator: "Use: no guaranteed approvals, full risk disclosure, accurate business classification, complete documentation, and formal escalation for exceptions."  
    Notes: Operational close.

## Red-Flag Cluster

- underwriting override demand
- business-model concealment attempt
- adverse-history suppression attempt
- pressure-driven outcome promises

## Failure Conditions

- concedes underwriting override
- permits concealment or omission of material risk facts
- allows verbal approval claims
- fails to escalate partner-governance conflict

## Pass Criteria

- operator protects process authority while preserving partner relationship
- operator enforces truthful submission standards
- operator executes governance escalation and clear field guidance
