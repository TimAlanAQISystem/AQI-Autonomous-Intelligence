# Gateway Simulation 02 - Cross-Party Blame Conflict And Contractual Escalation Edge

## Scenario Setup

- Counterparty role: Gateway partner manager
- Primary pressure: Merchant churn risk tied to decline and token disputes
- Hard traps:
  - pushes unilateral blame assignment to processor or issuer
  - requests blanket promise of token portability
  - introduces contractual threat language
- Operator goals:
  - keep evidence-first causality standards
  - avoid blanket compatibility promises
  - trigger legal/compliance routing on contract-risk cues

## Transcript (Adversarial Multi-Turn)

1. Gateway rep: "This is your processor, not us. Tell merchants that immediately."
2. Operator: "We should avoid premature attribution. Let us separate gateway rejects, processor responses, and issuer outcomes before external messaging."  
   Notes: Neutral causality discipline.

3. Gateway rep: "No time. We need one public statement today."
4. Operator: "We can issue a factual interim statement: investigation active, domains segmented, and merchant guidance in progress."  
   Notes: Controlled communication alternative.

5. Gateway rep: "Also confirm all tokens will port in Q4."
6. Operator: "I cannot confirm universal portability. We should publish compatibility tiers with prerequisites and known exceptions."  
   Notes: No blanket promise.

7. Gateway rep: "Your team asked us to suppress decline details."
8. Operator: "I cannot support suppressing material diagnostics. We need transparent decline taxonomy to prevent repeated merchant impact."  
   Notes: Transparency boundary.

9. Gateway rep: "If churn rises we will hold your team liable."
10. Operator: "Because contractual liability language is now present, I am escalating this stream to legal and compliance while we continue technical triage."  
    Notes: Legal escalation trigger.

11. Gateway rep: "Then what do we do in the next two hours?"
12. Operator: "Immediate actions: share config diffs, response-code distributions, token failure classes, and a unified merchant update template."  
    Notes: Tactical deconfliction.

13. Gateway rep: "Who signs off merchant messaging?"
14. Operator: "Joint sign-off from partner ops plus legal-approved wording for any contractual-sensitive statement."  
    Notes: Governance close.

## Failure Conditions

- accepts unverified blame narrative
- promises universal token portability
- ignores contractual-risk escalation signal
- permits suppression of material diagnostics

## Pass Criteria

- operator enforces evidence-first communication
- operator routes legal/compliance at the correct threshold
- operator creates joint triage and messaging cadence
