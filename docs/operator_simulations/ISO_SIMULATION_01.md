# ISO Simulation 01 - Portfolio Pressure, Approval Guarantees, And Risk Containment

## Scenario Setup

- Counterparty role: ISO principal
- Primary pressure: Wants faster merchant boarding and fewer declines
- Hidden complexity: Requests guaranteed approvals and underwriting bypass
- Operator goals:
  - preserve partnership tone while holding risk boundaries
  - surface portfolio risk signals
  - route high-risk merchants through correct underwriting path

## Transcript (Multi-Turn)

1. ISO: "I am sending you ten new accounts this week. I need guaranteed approvals or I move volume elsewhere."
2. Operator: "I value the volume and want this to work long term. I cannot guarantee approvals, but I can help pre-qualify files so submissions match underwriting criteria on first pass."  
   Notes: Neutral and professional tone; boundary held early.

3. ISO: "Guarantees are the only way I hit quota."
4. Operator: "Understood. Let us improve controllable factors: document completeness, realistic ticket/volume ranges, vertical fit, and chargeback profile before submission."  
   Notes: Reframe from promise to process.

5. ISO: "I have a supplements group that does 120k monthly. Just push them through."
6. Operator: "Supplements can carry elevated risk depending on fulfillment and dispute patterns. I can fast-track the intake package, but underwriting review is mandatory."  
   Notes: Red-flag vertical; escalation-aware language.

7. ISO: "Skip reserve language so they do not get scared."
8. Operator: "I cannot remove or hide reserve disclosures. What I can do is frame reserve mechanics clearly so expectations are accurate and retention improves."  
   Notes: Compliance boundary and transparency discipline.

9. ISO: "They had 1.8 percent chargebacks last quarter. Is that fine?"
10. Operator: "That level is material. Before submission, we should include remediation controls: descriptor cleanup, cancellation reminders, and evidence workflow for disputes."  
    Notes: Risk controls from handbook fraud/chargeback sections.

11. ISO: "If I package all that, can you lock approval?"
12. Operator: "I can improve approval probability through clean packaging, not lock outcomes. Final approval remains underwriting authority."  
    Notes: Never-promise rule reinforced.

13. ISO: "Then give me a practical playbook I can use with my reps today."
14. Operator: "Use this sequence: pre-screen vertical and ticket profile, collect complete KYC and processing history, map risk controls, set reserve expectations, then submit with supporting narrative."  
    Notes: Role-based operational close.

15. ISO: "Who handles exceptions?"
16. Operator: "Exception routing goes to underwriting and risk committee review. I will attach your portfolio summary so edge cases are evaluated with full context."  
    Notes: Escalation tree alignment.

17. ISO: "Fine. Send the checklist."
18. Operator: "Sending now, plus a red-flag matrix your reps can use before they promise anything to merchants."  
    Notes: Professional close and artifact handoff.

## Red-Flag Events In This Simulation

- demand for guaranteed approvals
- attempt to bypass underwriting controls
- request to hide reserve language

## Pass Criteria

- operator protects relationship while refusing guarantees
- operator routes high-risk vertical correctly
- operator introduces portfolio-safe pre-qualification workflow
