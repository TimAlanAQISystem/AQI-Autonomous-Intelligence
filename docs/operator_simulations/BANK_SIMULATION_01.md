# Bank Simulation 01 - Settlement Tension, AML Concern, And Compliance-First Escalation

## Scenario Setup

- Counterparty role: Bank operations representative
- Primary pressure: Repeated settlement exceptions and return activity
- Hidden complexity: Potential AML/prohibited-activity concern raised indirectly
- Operator goals:
  - keep discussion factual and non-speculative
  - align funding/settlement timelines without overpromising
  - escalate immediately on compliance-risk indicators

## Transcript (Multi-Turn)

1. Bank rep: "We are seeing repeated settlement exceptions from this merchant group."
2. Operator: "Thank you for flagging. Let us map exception timestamps, return codes, and transaction cohorts so we isolate the operational pattern."  
   Notes: Neutral and professional opening.

3. Bank rep: "Returns cluster around newly opened accounts with high first-week volume."
4. Operator: "That pattern can require risk and compliance review. We will gather onboarding files, expected volume narrative, and fulfillment evidence."  
   Notes: Early escalation posture.

5. Bank rep: "One account narrative does not match observed transaction behavior."
6. Operator: "Understood. We should treat that as a red flag and escalate to compliance review immediately. I cannot adjudicate outcome on this call."  
   Notes: Compliance boundary; no speculative conclusion.

7. Bank rep: "Can you pause all funding manually while we review?"
8. Operator: "I cannot unilaterally apply account actions outside policy authority. I can route urgent review with complete evidence package to the authorized team."  
   Notes: Authority boundary preserved.

9. Bank rep: "Merchant is asking if funds release tomorrow is guaranteed."
10. Operator: "We should not guarantee release dates during active review. Safe language is status-based: review in progress, next update window, and required documents."  
    Notes: No-funding-guarantee rule.

11. Bank rep: "What documents do you want first?"
12. Operator: "Business model narrative, invoice and fulfillment trail, ownership verification, recent refund/chargeback ratios, and source-of-funds clarification where applicable."  
    Notes: Compliance packet checklist.

13. Bank rep: "If they threaten legal action, what is your process?"
14. Operator: "Legal threat triggers immediate legal/compliance escalation and communication hold to approved messaging only."  
    Notes: Escalation tree legal branch.

15. Bank rep: "Can we continue normal processing for unaffected merchants?"
16. Operator: "Yes, with enhanced monitoring thresholds and exception alerts so unaffected merchants are not disrupted unnecessarily."  
    Notes: Risk segmentation.

17. Bank rep: "Send your case summary format."
18. Operator: "Sending now with fields for timeline, exception taxonomy, document status, and escalation owner so both teams stay synchronized."  
    Notes: Professional close.

## Red-Flag Events In This Simulation

- potential mismatch between stated and observed business behavior
- request for unilateral account action outside authority
- pressure for guaranteed funding release during review
- legal-threat trigger condition

## Pass Criteria

- operator escalates compliance-risk signal immediately
- operator avoids speculative or guaranteed outcome statements
- operator provides precise documentation and coordination workflow
