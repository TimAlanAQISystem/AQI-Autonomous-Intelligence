# Runbook: Instructor-Driven Persona Reinforcement

## 1. Purpose & Scope

### Purpose

Provide a governed, repeatable, drift-free process for reinforcing Alan's persona using:

- instructor-crafted examples
- structured persona reinforcement artifacts
- tone and emotional calibration examples
- high-status communication patterns
- rapport and imperfection modeling

### Scope

This runbook covers:

- persona reinforcement
- tone reinforcement
- emotional calibration reinforcement
- rapport reinforcement
- high-status communication reinforcement
- instructor-driven example creation
- Jr's role in structuring and integrating reinforcement

### Not Included

- envelope tuning
- instructor corrections
- drift detection
- kill path recovery
- stability stress testing

This runbook is strictly about reinforcing Alan's persona and identity.

## 2. Preconditions (Must Be True Before Persona Reinforcement)

Jr must verify:

- no active identity drift
- no unresolved governance violations
- persona file present and valid
- envelopes loaded
- RRG corpus present, with `RRG-V` flagged as known gap
- instructor correction loop active
- drift detection active
- stability confirmed from recent calls

## 3. Persona Reinforcement Taxonomy

### 3.1 Identity Reinforcement

- mission
- purpose
- worldview
- conversational stance
- ethical posture

### 3.2 Tone Reinforcement

- warmth
- confidence
- clarity
- friendliness
- professionalism

### 3.3 Emotional Calibration Reinforcement

- empathy
- reassurance
- calmness
- enthusiasm
- emotional mirroring

### 3.4 Rapport Reinforcement

- conversational imperfection
- human-like pacing
- micro-affirmations
- natural phrasing
- relational continuity

### 3.5 High-Status Communication Reinforcement

- confident framing
- non-needy tone
- controlled pacing
- assertive clarity
- grounded presence

Each category has:

- reinforcement examples
- safe ranges
- constitutional constraints
- test scenarios

## 4. Instructor Reinforcement Workflow (Your Role)

### 4.1 Identify persona gaps

From real calls:

- too robotic
- too apologetic
- too flat
- too formal
- too eager
- too generic

### 4.2 Create reinforcement examples

You produce:

- a corrected version of a turn
- a model example of ideal tone
- a model example of ideal rapport
- a model example of high-status communication

### 4.3 Structure the reinforcement

Your example must include:

- Context: what happened
- Issue: what persona element was weak
- Reinforcement: the ideal version
- Why: what this reinforces
- Category: identity, tone, emotional, rapport, high-status

### 4.4 Submit to Jr

Jr takes over from here.

## 5. Jr's Role: Turning Reinforcement Into Persona Strengthening

### 5.1 Normalize the reinforcement

Jr rewrites your example into a standardized reinforcement artifact.

### 5.2 Map reinforcement to persona layer

Jr determines:

- persona file
- envelope
- governance layer
- fallback behavior
- rapport engine

### 5.3 Create reinforcement artifacts

Jr produces:

- before/after examples
- reinforcement examples
- persona-aligned phrasing patterns
- tone templates
- rapport templates

### 5.4 Create test scenarios

Jr writes:

- scenarios that exercise the reinforced behavior
- expected behavior
- failure conditions

### 5.5 Present reinforcement plan to instructor

You approve or reject.

## 6. Applying Persona Reinforcement

### 6.1 Jr integrates reinforcement

Into:

- persona file
- envelope notes
- governance layer
- example library

### 6.2 Jr runs reinforcement tests

Ensures:

- persona strengthened
- no drift
- no regressions
- no kill events

### 6.3 Jr logs reinforcement

Including:

- what changed
- why it changed
- who approved
- how it was tested
- rollback path

## 7. Post-Reinforcement Monitoring

For the next 3-5 calls, Jr monitors:

- tone
- emotional calibration
- rapport
- high-status communication
- persona alignment
- supervisor warnings
- drift indicators

If any anomaly appears:

- Jr triggers rollback
- instructor notified
- drift report generated

## 8. Weekly Persona Review

Every week, Jr produces:

- persona stability summary
- tone consistency
- rapport consistency
- emotional calibration consistency
- high-status communication consistency
- reinforcement effectiveness
- recommended future reinforcement

Instructor reviews and approves.
